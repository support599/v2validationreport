About Our Founders

Founder Photo Placeholder

Alex and Leila Hormozi are co-founders and managing partners of Acquisition.com. In four years, the portfolio grew to over $250M+ in annual revenue. Before Acquisition.com they founded Gym Launch, Prestige Labs, and ALAN. In 2021, a private equity sponsor purchased Gym Launch and Prestige Labs for $46.2M. And in that same year, a strategic buyer bought ALAN in an all stock deal.

Their mission is to make real business education accessible to everyone. To do that, they strive to build a business that improves the lives of everyone it comes in contact with. They built a culture of praise over punishment to make it easy for employees to give and receive feedback. Alex and Leila have an audience of over 12M+ who subscribe to their business content across social media.
