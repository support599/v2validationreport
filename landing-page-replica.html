About Our Founder

I started a software company from the ground up with a few partners — and went through hell figuring out marketing. Cheap freelancers. Expensive agencies that overpromised and underdelivered. None of it moved the needle.

So we brought everything in-house. I found an advisor to teach me marketing myself — and within a short window we were closing sales, seeing 3X to 10X return on ad spend. That painful, expensive lesson became the G5 Framework: a curated system built on Go High Level and the best AI tools available, made for service-based owners who've spent their careers heads-down as operators — right as a new wave of competitors starts winning with LSA and Meta's paid media.

I'm Angel John Renteria. If you already have a service-based business with proof of concept — clients, testimonials, results — you don't need to start over. You just need the systems. That's exactly what I build.
